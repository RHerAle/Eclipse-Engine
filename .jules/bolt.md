## 2026-10-12 - Skipping zero-contribution segments in radial quadrature
**Learning:** In the radial quadrature for evaluating eclipsed flux obscuration (`fluxObscuration`), iterating over the entire solar radius uniformly requires computing expensive Math.pow/Math.acos operations even for annulus segments that fall completely outside the overlapping region (where `phi = 0` and thus `f = 0`). Because the integration steps are independent, identifying the minimum and maximum boundaries analytically using `asin` allows us to skip zero-contribution iterations.
**Action:** When performing radial integrals involving overlapping circles, geometrically precompute the domain where the overlap is non-zero, and restrict the loop iterations to that window. In `fluxObscuration`, this provided roughly a ~4x reduction in run time.

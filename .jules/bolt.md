## 2024-05-19 - Batching Geometric Evaluations over Spectra
**Learning:** In calculations of limb darkening (where flux obscuration is calculated per wavelength using Simpson's rule over alpha), the geometric intersection between the sun and moon is extremely expensive (computing arc-cosines) but is constant across all limb-darkening coefficients (alphas). Computing these independently for 14 interpolation nodes was doing redundant math 14 times per time step.
**Action:** Always evaluate geometry that depends purely on physical placement (e.g. `rho`, `sep`, `rs`, `rm`) outside the loop or pass it down to a batched function that takes all coefficients (`alphas`), computing the geometry once and applying the differing polynomial terms in an inner loop. This gave roughly a 25-30% performance boost to radiometry runs.
